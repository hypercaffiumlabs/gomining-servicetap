Daily rewards: TH, GOMINING or BTC

Each day, GoMining sends your mining reward to TH on one of your miners, to GOMINING tokens in your wallet, or as BTC (GoMining → Settings → Reinvestment). ServiceTap can keep one of these until you change it, or alternate between two or three, e.g. 5 days TH, then 5 days GOMINING, then 3 days BTC, repeating. It can also keep your mining pool set. Changes happen in the same nightly run as the service button, before the morning payout.

Your GitHub sign-in happens directly with GitHub; this page never sees your GoMining login or password.
Settings are saved as plain (non-secret) Variables in your repo, visible under Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → Variables.
